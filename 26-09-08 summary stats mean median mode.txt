Why do we need summary stats?
makes datasets digestible
You can set goals, and standards

Summary stats:
For number
Mean, Median, Mode

Mean: take sum, divided by number of items
set: 1 2 3 4 5 6
Mean is sum 21 over number 6 = 3.5
weighted mean : the mean is extrated after considering the relative weight of all items

Median

Median - middle number
The data value in the middle of the list. If you have an even number
of data points, take the average between those numbers
Set 0, 1, 3, 12, 14, the median is 3

Mode - the most common number
set 0, 2, 4, 4, 5 , 7 the mode is 4
You can get multiple modes
if the existing modes are equal,
set 0, 2, 2, 2, 4, 4, 4, 5, 7
mode is 2 and 4
works for categorical data, since no math






Misleading summary stats
Outliers - a massive value can skew averages
1, 2, 3, 3, 3, 800. The average is 135.33~, not very useful
You can remove the outlier, take the median.

high-low split - even number of values can cause an anomalous median
12 13 14 100 110 130 mean is 57 because even number of values


8, 12, 12, 15, 16, 17, 18, 20, 21, 22, 23, 24, 25, 25, 47
mean is 20.33~, mode is 12 and 25, median is 20
