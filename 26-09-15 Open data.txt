Define
range difference between highest and lowest value
IQR - Interqartile range difference between Q1 and Q3

Variance - how to calculate: Find the mean
find the difference of each value from the mean
take the average of those values

Standard deviation the square root of variance
The typical difference between values

Quartiles - and percentiles: the three values at quarterly points
25% 50% and 75% points
50% is the median

Histogram - a gapless bar chart, it organizes instances into intervals called bins
it shows the number of values in each bin


Outlier - an unusally large or small value
Identified with the 1.5 IQR rule
Take Q1 -1.5*IQR and Q3 + 1.5*IQR
these form the lower and upper bound 
The represent an error, or an extreme

Dataset eval checklist
Purpose & relevance
credibility
Timeliness - is it relevant to the current date? is it outdated?
Granularity - Is the level of detail appropriate
Field clarity - Do you understand what each variable actually means
Completeness - are important values missing?
Consistency - is the data internally consistent
Bias & limitation - what does the dataset not capture?
Accessibility & Format - is the data usable with available tools?
Legal & ethical - are there restrictions on use or sharing?
