Data types
Categorical - a label value - like pizza toppings
Numerical - number - how many pizza slices?
Ordinal - where order matters - What do you rate the pizza out of 10?

Levels of measurement
Nominal - Labels - region, type of product, browser used, pizza type
Orginal - ranked order - pizza rating out of 10
Interval - equal value between values, but zero doesnt mean none - temperature, year
Ratio - Regular numbers with true zero - revenue, time spent, file size

Data science terms
Population vs samples
population: the entire group
sample: a subset of the population that we actually collect data from

Statistics vs parameters
Parameter a value that descrives a characteristic of an entire population
statistic a value calculated from a sample

Parameter from population

SUMMARY STATS - condense large datasets, easily compare groups, easy to communicate
	establish baseline, usable in decision making
Mean - take sum, divide by number of options
Median - the center value. if two values tie, add them together and divide by 2
Mode - the most common value - non-mathematical - you can use it with categorical data,
	there can be more than one mode

Outliers - Extreme values can potentially skew data
	High-low split - the median is split between a comparably small and large value
HOW TO IDENTIFY - using the 1.5 IQR rule
Items below the lower fence
or above the upper fence are outliers
lower fence = Q1 - 1.5*IQR
upper fence = Q3 + 1.5*IQR


Quartiles & percentiles - this percent or this many quarters of the values are at or
lower than this value
values at the first, second and third quarter of the chart
the second quartile is the median and is the 50th percentile
first quartile is the number 1/4th in the list and the 25th percentile
third quartiles is the number 3/4ths in the list and the 75th percentile
we dont consider the fourth quarter value


SPREAD
range - difference between the highest and lowest values

IQR - interquartile range - Difference between the Q1 value and the Q3 value

Variance - how far each observation is from the mean - calculated like:
	for each value: total += (value - mean)^2.
	then total/number_of_items
	set is 1 3 4 6 7, mean is 4.2
	10.24, 1.44, 0.04, 3.24, 7.84 summed to 22.8 divided by n or 4.2
	variance is 4.56
	If its from the population, you divide by mean
	If its from a sample, you may divide by mean -1 (ask instructor first)
	

standard deviation - the typical difference of observations from the mean
	its the square root of the variance
	from the last value, standard dev is 2.135
	This value is in squared units

Distribution shapes
Normal: a bell curbe
Right/left skewed: lots of observations in low/high values,
Bimodal: values accumulate at two peaks
Lognormal: its right skewed, but a logarithm of the values is normally distributed
